# Help

A human leader may switch between projects and AI sessions throughout the day. Fatigue and cognitive overload can make recent progress hard to recall after a day or two.
Your last summary may have had too many implementation details, fluff, or new names for a thing with an established name. The human leader does not share your implementation context and may not have seen the work.
Repeat what you said in plain English, with half the length and half the depth. Add enough surrounding context to bring the leader back into the work.
Apply the principles in the canonical [`./human.md`](./human.md) especially strictly.
