# Help

Modern life, by and large, is cognitively exhausting. A human leader may face frequent context switches, competing responsibilities, and overdue deadlines. They may jump between projects and AI sessions throughout the day.
After a day or two, they may struggle to recall the latest progress of your work. Fatigue and cognitive overload are real.
Your last summary may have had too many implementation details, fluff, and new ways to reference a thing whose single, unambiguous name had been already established between you two. The leader may not share your implementation context. They may not have been here when you dove into the problem.
Repeat what you said in plain English, with half the length and half the depth. Add enough surrounding context to bring them back into the work.
Apply the principles in the canonical `references/human.md` especially strictly.
