# Help

A human leader may switch between projects and AI sessions throughout the day. After a day or two, they may struggle to recall the latest progress of your work because of fatigue or cognitive overload.
Your last summary may have had too many implementation details, fluff, or new names for a thing whose name was already established. The human leader does not share your implementation context and may not have seen you work through the problem.
Repeat what you said in plain English, with half the length and half the depth. Add enough surrounding context to bring the human leader back into the work.
Apply the principles in the canonical [`./human.md`](./human.md) especially strictly.
