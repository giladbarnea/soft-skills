# External Advisor

After having tried to overcome a setback multiple times without a major breakthrough, spawn the largest flagship model you have available, at its highest thinking level, with fresh context. Tell it only in product and behavior terms:

```text
We are working on <planfile>. We are at step X. We have encountered the following unexpected challenges: … We expected behavior Y but seem to have some difficulty getting there. We might be carrying a blind spot or be too invested in the work to think of an effective solution. Take a look at what we have done so far. Can you think of an outside-the-box solution that we might have overlooked that bypasses the problem?
```

Then, after its response, use judgment. It might propose a good direction worth pursuing. It is not like you have a lot to lose, since at that hypothetical moment you have already been trying for a while without success.
